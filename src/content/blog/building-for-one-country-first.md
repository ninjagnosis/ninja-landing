A product built for one country can be better for that country than a product built for everyone. That is the case for starting narrow, and for a group like ours it is a deliberate choice.

## What depth gives you

- **Language and habit.** Software that speaks the language people use at work, and follows the way they already do things, is used sooner.
- **Rules.** Local law, local forms and local payment habits are real constraints. Meeting them well is a barrier to those who treat them as an afterthought.
- **Proximity.** You can meet your customers. That is worth more than a research report.

## What it costs you

A single market is a ceiling. Be honest about how large it is, and decide in advance what would make you look further: a product that works abroad with little change, or a customer that asks you to follow them.

## Keep the option open

Build so that language, currency and legal text are data, not code spread through the product. Then going further is a decision, not a rewrite.
